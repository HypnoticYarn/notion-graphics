Day by Design • Email graphics

The icon guide shows every email action. The email graphics guide shows the headers, spot illustrations, footers, and dividers by filename.

Inside the folders

Icons — 24 symbols, each supplied as a transparent 96 × 96 PNG and a scalable SVG. Use PNG in an email and SVG in the planner app. Display at 24 × 24 or 32 × 32. Pair clickable icons with a visible label such as “Reply all” or “Schedule send.”

Headers — Three 1200 × 300 JPG designs for a 600-pixel-wide email. Airmail margins also comes with “Day by Design” already typeset. Place the subject or issue title underneath as live email text.

Illustrations — Six transparent 960 × 960 PNGs. Try The morning edition in a daily digest, Scheduled dispatch in a send-later confirmation, or A lighter inbox when there are no messages to review. Display at about 160–280 pixels wide.

Footers and Dividers — Three of each, supplied as transparent PNGs. Display footers at 600 × 67 and dividers at 600 × 42, scaling proportionally for narrow screens. Place these on Paper (#FFFFFF) or Daylight (#F7F8F3). Keep unsubscribe and email preferences as live links.

Palette — The original Day by Design palette, with a visual swatch sheet and named hex codes.

For an accessible email, give the titled header the alt text “Day by Design.” Use empty alt text for decorative flourishes when the adjacent text already conveys the information.
